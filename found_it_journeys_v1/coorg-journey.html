Coorg: Mist, Coffee & Quiet Mornings

A slower journey through coffee country, forest roads and green hills — made for travellers who want a few quiet days away from the noise.

Morning in the hills

Start early and let the landscape set the pace. Coorg is best experienced slowly: misty roads, plantation landscapes and long breakfasts create the feeling of being somewhere far from routine.

Coffee country

Coffee is part of Coorg's identity. A plantation visit can turn a simple trip into a sensory journey — from the plants and shade trees to the stories behind the cup.
