Udaipur: A Sunset That Stayed Forever

Water, old stone and warm evening light come together in one of Rajasthan's most atmospheric cities.

A city around water

Udaipur's lakes shape the experience. Give yourself time to walk, look across the water and notice how the old city changes as the light moves.

Old streets and old stories

The city's historic areas reward slow exploration. Instead of rushing between attractions, make the journey about small discoveries — architecture, courtyards, markets and views.
