Coorg: Mist, Coffee & Quiet Mornings

A slower way to spend a few days among green hills, coffee estates and rain.

Coorg, also known as Kodagu, is the kind of destination where the journey itself becomes part of the experience. Hills, forests, coffee plantations and winding roads create a slower rhythm than the cities below.

Start with the landscape

Instead of trying to fill every hour, spend the morning outside. Walk through a coffee-growing area, stop at a viewpoint or simply follow the road through the hills.

The FOUND IT! challenge

This is where the journey becomes part of the game. Before travelling, choose a FOUND IT! model representing Coorg. Carry it with you and look for a real location that matches your model.

When you find it, take your photograph with the model. That photograph becomes your proof that you actually FOUND IT!
