Why solo travel changes you

There is a particular kind of freedom that appears when nobody else is deciding what the next hour should look like.

You start listening to yourself

Solo travel removes many of the small negotiations that normally shape a trip. You choose when to wake up, where to stop, what to eat and when to simply sit somewhere beautiful.

That freedom can feel unfamiliar at first. Then it becomes one of the most memorable parts of the journey. A quiet road, an unplanned café or a viewpoint you nearly skipped can become your favourite part of the day.

At FOUND IT!, that is where the idea of finding a place becomes more than reaching a destination. You carry a physical model, search for the real place and create a memory around the moment you finally find it.
