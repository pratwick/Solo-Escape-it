Collecting moments, not things

A good trip rarely fits into a suitcase. What stays is usually a collection of small moments.

The memory is the souvenir

A sunset from a roadside viewpoint, a conversation with a local, rain arriving just as you reach a hilltop or the feeling of finally finding a place you had been searching for can stay with you long after the journey ends.

That is why physical objects can be powerful when they represent an experience. A small model of a destination is not the destination itself; it is a prompt to remember the adventure of finding it.

FOUND IT! takes that idea and turns it into a social travel game: carry the model, find the real-world place, photograph the moment and share that you FOUND IT!
