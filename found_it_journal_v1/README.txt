INSTALL:
1. Copy the contents of index-section.html into your main index.html where the Journal section belongs.
2. Keep the found_it_journal_v1 folder beside index.html.
3. The four journal cards link to the four article pages.
4. The article pages link back to ../index.html#journal.
5. No extra CSS file is required for the article pages; their styling is self-contained.
