The beauty of slow mornings

Some trips are remembered not for how much you saw, but for how little you had to rush.

Leave room for the morning

A slow morning changes the rhythm of a journey. Instead of immediately checking a list of attractions, you can watch the light change, have breakfast without a clock and notice the sounds of the place around you.

In destinations such as Kerala's backwaters, the hills of Coorg or the quieter valleys of Himachal Pradesh, this slower rhythm can become the experience itself.

FOUND IT! is designed around that feeling: choose somewhere meaningful, take your model with you and turn the search for the real place into part of the journey.
