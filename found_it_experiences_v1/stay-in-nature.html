Stay in Nature

Choose places where the landscape becomes part of the experience.

A great stay can change the way you experience a destination. Instead of treating accommodation as somewhere to sleep, FOUND IT! turns it into part of the journey.

Choose the setting

Look for cabins, homestays, forest retreats and other stays that put you close to the landscape. The goal is not luxury for its own sake; it is a memorable relationship with the place.

Make it part of the game

Take your FOUND IT! physical model with you. During the stay, find a real location that matches the model, photograph the moment and keep it as your FOUND IT! proof.
