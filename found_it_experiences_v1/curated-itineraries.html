Curated Itineraries

Slow travel plans built around meaningful places rather than rushing through a checklist.

A good itinerary should leave room for discovery. Instead of packing every hour with attractions, a curated route connects a few meaningful places and gives you enough time to actually experience them.

Travel with intention

Build days around a small number of destinations, scenic routes, local food and time outdoors. Leave space for an unexpected stop or a place you discover along the way.

Turn the itinerary into FOUND IT!

Each route can become a sequence of FIND IT! missions. Carry the corresponding physical model, search for the real place, capture the proof and continue to the next challenge.
