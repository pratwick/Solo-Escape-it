Local Experiences

Taste, learn and participate in the stories that make a place feel real.

The fastest way to understand a destination is often to experience it through the people who live there. Food, craft, markets and everyday traditions reveal details that a checklist of attractions can miss.

Go beyond sightseeing

Try a local cooking experience, visit a market, meet a maker or learn a regional tradition. Choose experiences that are connected to the community and the character of the place.

Add the FOUND IT! layer

Carry your model while exploring. When you discover the real-world location represented by it, take the FOUND IT! photograph and turn an ordinary travel memory into a game moment.
