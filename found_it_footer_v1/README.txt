FOUND IT! — Footer V1

FILES
-----
1. index-footer.html
   Ready-to-paste footer section for index.html.

2. footer-extra.css
   Optional small enhancements only. Your existing footer CSS can remain unchanged.

3. README.txt
   Installation instructions.

INSTALLATION
------------
1. Open your main index.html.
2. Find your current <footer id="about">...</footer>.
3. Replace it with the complete contents of index-footer.html.
4. You do NOT need footer-extra.css if your current footer already looks correct.
5. The Explore links point to existing sections:
   #destinations
   #journeys
   #experiences
   #journal

IMPORTANT
---------
The "Coming soon" and Resources links are prepared as anchor links so they can become
real sections later. If those IDs do not yet exist in index.html, clicking them will
not move the page. They are intentionally ready for future FOUND IT! sections.

CONNECT LINKS
-------------
Instagram and YouTube currently open their platform homepages because no official
FOUND IT! social profile URLs were provided.

The Email link currently uses:
hello@foundit.example

Change that address to your real FOUND IT! email before publishing.

The Newsletter link goes to:
#newsletter
